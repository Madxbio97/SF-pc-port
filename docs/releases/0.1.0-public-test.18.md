# Syphon Filter PC 0.1.0-public-test.18

Windows x64 public-test candidate built on 2026-08-02.

## Highlights

- Restored PS1-authored HUD and letterbox timing for radio conversations,
  including reliable fast-skip behavior and display-rate smoothing over the
  original 20 Hz game logic. The visible transition now settles 15% faster.
- Reworked SVD night vision to preserve scene fog and gradients, restore scope
  markings and motion trails, rotate billboard sprites while aiming, and clear
  the green filter immediately on exit.
- Restored the viral detector's authored first-person sight and pulsing marker;
  level geometry, boxes and GMD receive the retail orange semitransparent
  material, while enemy models remain unchanged.
- Restored `SCOPED.TIM` with the sniper scope's specialized PS1
  STP/semitransparency blend, including its dark gradient and the dark regions
  around the crosshair.
- Removed alternating framebuffer-page flicker during presentation transitions.
- Hid the FPS counter by default; press F6 to show or hide it.
- Reduced in-level FMV startup latency, restored gameplay-FMV fades and fixed
  mission-briefing audio-clock startup failures.
- Renamed the launcher language option to `Русский`.
